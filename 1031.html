1031 exchanges, without missing a deadline

A 1031 exchange lets you sell an investment property and roll the proceeds into another one while deferring capital gains tax. The clock starts the day you close, and it doesn’t stop.

How an exchange runs

  1. Line up a qualified intermediary before you sell

    The sale proceeds go to the intermediary, not to you. Touching the money can end the exchange.

  2. Sell the property you’re giving up

    The closing date starts both deadlines.

  3. Identify replacement property within 45 days

    In writing, to your intermediary. The rules limit how many properties you can name.

  4. Close on the replacement within 180 days

    Or by your tax return due date for that year, including extensions, if that comes first.

My part is the real estate: selling yours for what it’s worth and finding replacements that fit both the deadline and your goals. That includes homes and small rentals in west Las Vegas, and commercial property such as triple-net leased buildings or apartment buildings when an exchange calls for it.
