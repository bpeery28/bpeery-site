Helping people with the biggest investment of their lives has always been my passion. Now that investment is their home.

For 16 years I managed investment portfolios. The best part was never the markets. It was helping people protect and grow what mattered most to them.

A home is the biggest investment most people will ever make, and it deserves the same care, the same attention to detail and the same honesty.

The short version

I spent 30 years in the markets, 16 of them as a senior vice president and portfolio manager at Hennessy Advisors, where I managed $3.5 billion in fund assets and led a team of 16 portfolio managers. I was the firm’s main media contact, with regular appearances on CNBC, Bloomberg and Fox Business.

For more than 30 years I’ve also bought, owned and managed my own investment property through my family office, Peery Properties: single-family rentals in Nevada, California and Florida, and triple-net leased commercial buildings in Alberta, Canada. I’ve been the landlord, the one approving the roof repair and the one negotiating the lease, and I bring that to every buyer and seller I work with.

Now I help people buy and sell homes in west Las Vegas’s gated golf and trail communities, from Red Rock Country Club and The Summit Club to Canyon Gate, where I live. I’m here because of what’s around them: mountain biking at Cottonwood Valley and from the trailhead by The Summit Club, ski days at Lee Canyon, trail time at Red Rock with Cooper, my golden retriever, a few rounds of golf, and time at Desert Sportsman’s, the members-only range where I shoot.
