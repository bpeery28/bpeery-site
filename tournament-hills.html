Tournament Hills

A guard-gated community in The Hills village of Summerlin, set along the private TPC Summerlin course, home of the PGA Tour’s Las Vegas stop.

  • Guard-gatedGate
  • On TPC SummerlinGolf

Living here

Custom and semi-custom homes, many on the course. Club membership at TPC Summerlin is separate from owning a home.

Getting out

The Summerlin trail network runs nearby, Red Rock Canyon is a short drive west, and Lee Canyon is under an hour away.

Summer heat is real on the west side: plan dog hikes and rides for early morning and carry water.
