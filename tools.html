Tax and insurance follow the price until you type your own. Tax: taxable value at about 80% of price, times Nevada’s 35% assessment ratio, times the 2026–27 district rate ($3.2782 per $100, City of Las Vegas). New owners must claim the 3% cap, and real bills often run lower; check the parcel with the Clark County Treasurer. Insurance: about $4.35 per $1,000 of rebuild cost (Las Vegas average), with rebuild at about 60% of price. Get real quotes.
