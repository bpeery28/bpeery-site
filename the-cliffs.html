Regency at The Cliffs

The guard-gated neighborhood of Summerlin’s Cliffs village: Regency by Toll Brothers, an age-qualified (55 and older) community of about 430 homes near Hualapai Way and the 215.

  • Guard-gated (staffed gate)Gate
  • No course inside; the Summerlin trail system runs throughGolf

Living here

Single- and two-story homes from Toll Brothers around a private clubhouse of more than 16,000 sq ft, with the association covering the staffed gate and amenities. It is the only neighborhood in The Cliffs with a staffed gate; the rest of the village is gated without a guard.

Getting out

The Summerlin trail system runs through The Cliffs, with Oak Leaf Park’s pickleball courts close by and Red Rock Canyon a short drive northwest.

Summer heat is real on the west side: plan dog hikes and rides for early morning and carry water.
