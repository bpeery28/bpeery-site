The Cliffs

Summerlin’s southern village along Hualapai Way near the 215, opened in 2015, with about 1,700 homes in gated neighborhoods built from 2016 to 2022.

  • Gated neighborhoods; Regency is staff-gatedGate
  • No course inside; the Summerlin trail system runs throughGolf

Living here

Ten or so neighborhoods from Toll Brothers, Pulte, Woodside, Lennar, Tri Pointe, Taylor Morrison and Richmond American, in a desert-contemporary style. Most are gated; Regency by Toll Brothers is a staff-gated 55+ neighborhood with its own clubhouse. Each neighborhood has its own association on top of the Summerlin master fee.

Getting out

The Summerlin trail system runs through the village, with Oak Leaf Park’s pickleball courts and climbing tower close by and Red Rock Canyon a short drive northwest.

Summer heat is real on the west side: plan dog hikes and rides for early morning and carry water.
