The Ridges

A guard-gated Summerlin community known for custom homes on view lots, minutes from Downtown Summerlin and Red Rock Canyon.

  • Guard-gatedGate
  • Amara Golf Club (formerly Bear’s Best, now private) inside the gatesGolf

Living here

Enclaves range from semi-custom homes to large custom estates. Each enclave has its own rules on top of the master association, so the street matters as much as the address.

Getting out

Red Rock Canyon’s trailheads are a short drive west, and Amara Golf Club, the former Bear’s Best now going private, sits inside the gates with Red Rock Country Club close by.

Summer heat is real on the west side: plan dog hikes and rides for early morning and carry water.
