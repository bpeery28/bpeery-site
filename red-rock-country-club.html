Red Rock Country Club

A guard-gated club community in Summerlin built around two Arnold Palmer signature golf courses, with Red Rock Canyon just to the west.

  • Guard-gatedGate
  • Two Arnold Palmer signature coursesGolf

Living here

Homes range from golf-course villas to custom estates, many with fairway or mountain views. Club membership is separate from owning a home here, so ask about the current membership options before you buy.

Getting out

Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area is a short drive west, with trails where leashed dogs are welcome. The Cottonwood Valley mountain bike trails are out past Blue Diamond, and Lee Canyon is under an hour up the mountain.

Summer heat is real on the west side: plan dog hikes and rides for early morning and carry water.

Buying or selling in Red Rock Country Club?

Within one community, a golf-course home and an interior street are different markets. I price each one against the sales that actually compare. Ask for a home value or tell me what you’re looking for.
