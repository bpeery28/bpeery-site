Privacy notice

Effective November 6, 2026

This notice explains how Brian Peery, a Nevada real estate salesperson (NV Lic. #S.189680) with Realty ONE Group Summerlin, handles information collected through bpeery.com. It is provided under Nevada Revised Statutes Chapter 603A.

Information collected

Only what you choose to share when you fill out a form or email: your name, email address, phone number, property address and details, your plans, and any message you write. The forms open your own email app to send your message; the site itself does not store what you type. Calculator entries stay in your browser.

Who may receive it

Your information is never sold. It may be shared only as needed to help you, with: Realty ONE Group Summerlin, which supervises Brian Peery and keeps transaction records; the email, calendar and phone services used to reply to you; with your direction, parties to your transaction such as lenders, escrow and title companies, inspectors, qualified intermediaries and other agents; and government agencies when the law requires it.

Reviewing or changing your information

Email brian@bpeery.com or call (415) 999-1044 to review, correct or delete what you’ve shared. Records the brokerage must keep by law may not be deletable.

Requests not to sell

Covered information is not sold. Nevada consumers may still send a request not to sell it to brian@bpeery.com.

Email

Marketing email goes only to people who asked for it, and every one has an unsubscribe link.

Third-party services

The site has no advertising trackers and sets no analytics cookies, and its fonts are hosted on the site itself, so no advertising network tracks your visit across websites. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which records pages viewed, the referring site, browser type and approximate country without cookies and without identifying you, under Cloudflare’s privacy policy. The booking calendar on the contact page is provided by Cal.com and loads only if you click “Show open times”; what you enter there goes to Cal.com and to Brian’s calendar, under Cal.com’s own privacy policy. The newsletter sign-up box on the home and contact pages is provided by beehiiv and loads from beehiiv’s servers; the email address you enter there goes to beehiiv, which sends the West Side Note on Brian’s behalf, under beehiiv’s own privacy policy. Maps on the tools page are provided by Google Maps and load only if you click “Show map”, under Google’s privacy policy. Links to Instagram, LinkedIn and research reports go to sites with their own privacy policies. The site does not respond to Do Not Track signals.

Changes

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date.
