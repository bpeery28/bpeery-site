Privacy Notice

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This notice explains how Brian Peery, a real estate salesperson (NV Lic. #S.189680) with Realty ONE Group Summerlin, handles information collected through bpeery.com (the “site”). It is provided under Nevada Revised Statutes Chapter 603A.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us, when you fill out a form, request a consultation, book a call, or email us:

  • Identifiers and contact details: name, email address, phone number
  • Property information: property address, property type, estimated value, and similar details you provide
  • Transaction details: 1031 exchange timing, goals, and any messages you send

The site’s forms open your own email program to send your message; the site itself does not store your form entries. Calculator inputs, game scores, and tracker entries stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

Who may receive your information

We do not sell your information. We may share it with the following categories of third parties, only as needed to respond to you or carry out a transaction you request:

  • Realty ONE Group Summerlin, the brokerage that supervises Brian Peery, which is required to keep transaction records
  • Email, calendar, and phone service providers we use to communicate with you
  • With your direction, parties to your transaction, such as qualified intermediaries, escrow and title companies, lenders, inspectors, and other licensees
  • Government agencies or others, when required by law

Reviewing or changing your information

To review, correct, or delete information you have given us, email brian@bpeery.com or call (415) 999-1044. We will respond within a reasonable time. Records the brokerage is legally required to keep may not be deletable.

Requests not to sell your information

We do not sell covered information. Nevada consumers may still submit a request directing us not to sell it by emailing brian@bpeery.com.

Third-party tracking

The site does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, and if you play the embedded video, YouTube will load. These services may receive your IP address and browser details, and they may collect information about your online activities over time and across different websites. Links to Instagram and LinkedIn are governed by those companies’ own privacy policies. The site does not respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this notice first.

Changes to this notice

If we make material changes, we will post the updated notice on this page with a new effective date.
