Siena

A guard-gated, age-restricted (55 and older) community in Summerlin, built around Siena Golf Club.

  • Guard-gatedGate
  • Siena Golf Club, open to public playGolf

Living here

Single-story homes are common here, many along the course, and the community’s clubhouse, amenities and rules are set by its association. The course is open to public play today, with annual memberships also offered; the club changed hands in recent years, so I’ll confirm the current access rules for any home you’re considering.

Getting out

Red Rock Canyon and the Summerlin trails are close by, and Lee Canyon is under an hour up the mountain.

Summer heat is real on the west side: plan dog hikes and rides for early morning and carry water.
