October 2026 · Special Issue
The 2026 Housing Supply Pipeline Rankings
  • Nevada ranks 49th of 51 in the 2026 housing supply pipeline rankings.
  • Units permitted Jan–Aug fell from 12,662 in 2025 to 8,986 in 2026 (−29%), the largest one-year decline in the rankings.
  • Nevada permitted about 1,123 homes a month against roughly 1,245 projected new households a month.
Read the report →
September 2026 · Vol. 3, No. 5
Short on the Strip: The Housing Permit Deficit in Las Vegas
  • Las Vegas metro residential permits fell 35% year over year through June 2026, the largest drop among the 55 U.S. metros over one million people.
  • 5,277 residential permits year to date through June 2026, 4,478 of them single-family.
  • Over five years, permits for buildings with five or more units fell 56%.
