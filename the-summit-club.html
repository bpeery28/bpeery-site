The Summit Club

A small, guard-gated luxury community in Summerlin with a private 18-hole course designed by Tom Fazio.

  • Guard-gatedGate
  • Private Tom Fazio courseGolf

Living here

Custom and estate homes on large lots, in one of the valley’s most private settings. Club membership and homeowner rules are specific to the community; I’ll walk you through both.

Getting out

Red Rock Canyon and the Summerlin trail network are close by, and Lee Canyon is under an hour away.

Summer heat is real on the west side: plan dog hikes and rides for early morning and carry water.
