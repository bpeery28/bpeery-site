The Preserve

A guard-gated Summerlin community of luxury estates with views toward Red Rock Canyon.

Living here

Larger homes on generous lots, chosen as much for the views and the trail access as for the house itself.

Getting out

This is a trail-first address: Red Rock Canyon and the Summerlin trail network are close, with golf a short drive away.

Summer heat is real on the west side: plan dog hikes and rides for early morning and carry water.

Buying or selling in The Preserve?

Within one community, a golf-course home and an interior street are different markets. I price each one against the sales that actually compare. Ask for a home value or tell me what you’re looking for.
