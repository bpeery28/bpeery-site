Canyon Gate Country Club

A guard-gated golf community at Sahara Avenue and Buffalo Drive, built around a private 18-hole Ted Robinson course.

  • Guard-gatedGate
  • Private 18-hole Ted Robinson courseGolf

Living here

Golf-course homes and quieter interior streets, close to Tivoli Village and Boca Park. Club membership is separate from owning a home and optional. I live here, so I know it street by street.

Getting out

The Red Rock Canyon visitor center is about a 16-minute drive, and the rest of the west side’s trails, ranges and slopes follow from there.

Summer heat is real on the west side: plan dog hikes and rides for early morning and carry water.

Buying or selling in Canyon Gate Country Club?

Within one community, a golf-course home and an interior street are different markets. I price each one against the sales that actually compare. Ask for a home value or tell me what you’re looking for.
